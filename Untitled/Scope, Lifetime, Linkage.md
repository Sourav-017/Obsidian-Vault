## **Scope and Duration**

- **Scope**: Defines where a variable can be accessed (local or global).
- **Duration**: Specifies the lifetime of a variable (automatic, static).

## **Scope, Duration, and Linkage**

- **Scope**: Refers to where a variable or function can be accessed.
- **Duration**: Specifies how long a variable persists during the program's execution.
- **Linkage**: Determines the visibility of variables or functions across translation units (internal or external linkage).
